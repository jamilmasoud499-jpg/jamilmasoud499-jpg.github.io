JARVIS Desktop

JARVIS is a Windows desktop assistant controlled by its authorized user through Telegram. Its TikTok integration connects the user’s TikTok account with their consent and lets them submit a video, review a caption and available privacy settings, then request posting.

How posting works

The bot receives a video from its authorized user, shows a review of the caption and TikTok account options, and sends the video to TikTok only after the user issues the publish command. TikTok controls the available posting permissions and may restrict public posting while the app is under review.

Availability

JARVIS runs on the user’s own computer. Features depend on Telegram, TikTok permissions and the local installation.
