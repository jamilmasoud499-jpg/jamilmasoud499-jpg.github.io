Service

JARVIS is a desktop assistant with a Telegram interface. Its authorized user may connect a TikTok account, prepare a video and request posting through the official TikTok integration, subject to TikTok permissions and limits.

Authorized content

Only submit content you own or are authorized to publish. You are responsible for your videos, captions, account settings and compliance with applicable platform rules.

Approval before posting

JARVIS shows a review of the video settings and requires a separate publish command. A posting attempt may fail or be restricted by TikTok.

Availability

The software runs on the user’s computer and depends on external services. Service availability is not guaranteed.
